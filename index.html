The heart of Edify

The vision

The Edify Gatherings is a retreat-style gathering for ministers of the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.

At each gathering, ministers share their experiences in ministry and learn together from the Scriptures. We ask questions and discuss them together, so that truth is revealed and understanding is brought to bear.

We pray, glean prophetically into the mind of God, and make room for the Holy Spirit to minister and speak: to the ministers gathered, to the Church, and about the coming move of God concerning the nations.
